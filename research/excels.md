<h1 style="color:rgb(5,135,67)">JWST EXCELS</h1>

The EXtragalactic Continuum and Emission Line Science survey is a *JWST* Cycle 2 spectroscopic program (ID: 3543, PI: A. C. Carnall, F. Cullen) targeted ~350 star-forming and quiescent galaxies with the JWST/NIRSpec medium resolution gratings, with the aim of obtaining deep spectra. Science goals included the formation and quenching mechanisms of quiescent galaxies, and the nebular, chemical, and ionizing properties of star-forming galaxies.
