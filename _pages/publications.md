## Co-author Papers

I have additionally co-authored a number of papers with my network of collaborators. A few recent highlights are listed below, while a full record can be found on [SciXplorer](https://tinyurl.com/tmstanton-scix).

- [*Spectroscopic Confirmation of a Large and Luminous Galaxy with Weak Emission Lines at z = 13.53*](https://scixplorer.org/abs/2026ApJ..1002..134D/abstract), Donnan et al. (2026).
- [*The AURORA Survey: Constraining Chemical Enrichment Pathways at Cosmic Noon with Argon Abundances*](https://scixplorer.org/abs/2026ApJ..1006...54F/abstract), Foley et al. (2026).
- [*The JWST EXCELS survey: A spectroscopic investigation of the ionizing properties of star-forming galaxies at 1<z<8*](https://scixplorer.org/abs/2026MNRAS.545f1995B/abstract), Begley et al. (2026).
- [*The JWST EXCELS survey: an extremely metal-poor galaxy at z = 8.271 hosting an unusual population of massive stars*](https://scixplorer.org/abs/2025MNRAS.540.2176C/abstract), Cullen et al. (2025).
- [*The JWST EXCELS survey: direct estimates of C, N, and O abundances in two relatively metal-rich galaxies at z ≃ 5*](https://scixplorer.org/abs/2025MNRAS.540.2991A/abstract), Arellano-Córdova et al. (2025).
