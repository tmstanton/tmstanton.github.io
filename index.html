Astrophysicist

Galaxy and Chemical Evolution

I am currently a postdoctoral research associate at the Institute for Astronomy at the University of Edinburgh, working with Ross McLure on the chemical and nebular properties of star-forming galaxies in the high-redshift Universe, and the theoretical modelling of galaxy nebular properties with photoionization modelling codes. Prior to this position, I completed a PhD at the Institute for Astronomy, University of Edinburgh, under the supervision of Fergus Cullen as part of the ERC/UKRI funded project 'ICE'.

Outreach

I have been involved in some outreach events with the University of Edinburgh, and plan to provide public interest talks on extragalactic astronomy.

Other Projects

I'm particularly interested in DEI initiatives around widening participation in Astronomy and Astrophysics.
